Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 July 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the application (“the App”) handles user information.

1. Information We Collect

The App does not collect, transmit, or share personal data.

  • No account registration is required
  • No personal identifiers (such as name, email, or phone number) are collected
  • No location data is collected
  • No analytics or tracking technologies are used

The App operates entirely on your device.

2. Local Data

Any data created while using the App (such as selected cities, time settings, or reminders) is stored locally on your device only and is not transmitted to any external servers.

3. System-Level Data

The App itself does not collect crash reports or diagnostic information. The platform provider (such as Apple or Google) may collect technical diagnostics according to its own privacy policies.

4. Calendar Access

TimeCross may request access to your device's calendar when you enable features that require calendar integration, such as starting reminders from a future date.

Calendar access is used solely to create, update, or remove calendar events required for these features.

Calendar data is processed only on your device and is not transmitted to our servers or shared with third parties.

TimeCross does not upload, store, analyze, or share the contents of your calendar.

5. Third-Party Services

The App does not use third-party services that collect user data.

6. Data Sharing

No user data is shared with third parties.

7. Children’s Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect any information from children under the age of 13.

8. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated revision date.
